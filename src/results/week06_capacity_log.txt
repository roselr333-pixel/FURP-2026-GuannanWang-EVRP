==============================================================================
FINDINGS
==============================================================================
  1. At the declared CAP=1000 the constraint is free for N in [8, 12, 16, 20, 30, 50] and binds in 1 instance(s) at N in [100]; a cap below the total demand binds for every larger size.
  2. When the cap binds, the truck-only variants are infeasible by construction and V2 removes enough demand to satisfy the cap in 0% of the binding cells (cap=400@N=50: 0%, cap=1000@N=100: 0%, cap=600@N=100: 0%, cap=400@N=100: 0%).
  3. The repair ceiling is the drone, not the search: V2 leaves 64.7% (N=8) to 97.2% (N=100) of the demand on the truck, so one serial drone only removes a modest share. In practice the capacity constraint behaves like a hard "total demand <= cap" bound.
  4. Capacity and energy are separate gates at N=100: V1/V2 are energy-feasible in 0%/0% of the instances, so the N=100 feasibility drop is not caused by capacity alone.
  5. Natural fixes: a load-aware LNS (destroy-repair that treats the cap as a hard constraint), or several drones, which multiply the demand that can leave the truck.
